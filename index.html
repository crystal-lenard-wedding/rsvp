Our Story

How It All Began

Our Story

🍋
Labor Day · September 2024

A bookstore, a bench, and a beginning

It started with a swipe. Lenard matched with Crystal on Hinge and wasted no time — he asked to meet in person, and on Labor Day 2024 they found themselves at a beautiful bookstore and café in Culver City, Los Angeles. They talked for hours on a colorful bench outside, moved on to lunch at Cava, and said goodbye with a warm hug that neither of them wanted to end. Everything else, as they say, is history.

A Year of Adventures · 2024 – 2025

The world, together — and then forever

When you meet the right person, everything moves at exactly the right speed. Within their first week as a couple they were already on a plane to Vancouver. A month later, China. Then the adventures kept coming — La Jolla, a snowy Christmas in Denver and Vail, the vast silence of Death Valley, Seattle in spring, the California redwoods, and a dozen places in between.

Then came August 2025 — a golden afternoon on Catalina Island, where Lenard got down on one knee. She said yes. 🌊 The rest of the year was one long celebration: Bainbridge Island, a full sweep of their favorite cities, and now — Santa Barbara on October 25th, 2026, surrounded by everyone they love most. We can't wait to share this day with you. 🍋
