Getting Here

Travel to Santa Barbara

Choose what works for your weekend. Once you're in Santa Barbara, our venue is Simpson House Inn at 121 E Arrellaga St.

By air

🛫

Fly into SBA

Santa Barbara Airport is the closest airport to the venue. Plan on about 13 minutes by car to Simpson House Inn.

About 13 min to the venue Airport info ↗
✈️

Fly into LAX

From Los Angeles International Airport, connect to US-101 north toward Santa Barbara. Allow about two hours in light traffic and extra time when roads are busy.

About 2 hr in light traffic Airport info ↗

By train or car

🚂

Pacific Surfliner

Amtrak's Pacific Surfliner connects LA Union Station with Santa Barbara. Check your train's schedule and arrange a ride from the station to the venue.

Santa Barbara station Train schedules ↗
🚗

Drive via US-101

Take US-101 north from LA or south from the Bay Area. Travel times vary significantly with traffic, so check your route before leaving.

Getting to the venue

🅿️

Parking nearby

Street parking around Simpson House Inn can be limited, and there is no dedicated guest parking lot. Leave extra time if you're driving.

Take a rideshare

A rideshare or taxi avoids the parking search. Use Simpson House Inn, 121 E Arrellaga St, Santa Barbara as your destination.
