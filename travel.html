Getting Here

Travel to Santa Barbara

Choose the route that fits your weekend best. Santa Barbara is easy by air, train, or a scenic coastal drive.

✈️
Fly into LAX
Los Angeles International Airport Arrival, followed by a scenic drive north along PCH or US-101.
~2 hr from LAX
🛫
Fly into SBA
Santa Barbara Airport (SBA) is served by several major airlines and is the closest airport to the venue, about 13 minutes from Simpson House Inn.
~13 min from SBA
🚂
Amtrak Surfliner
A beautiful coastal train from LA Union Station, with ocean cliffs along the way.
~2.5 hr from LA
🚗
Drive via US-101
From LA, head north through Ventura. From SF, head south through rolling hills and lemon groves.
~2 hr from LA · ~5.5 hr from SF
🅿️
Parking at Venue
Street parking is available around Simpson House Inn, but spaces may be limited. There is no dedicated guest parking lot, so rideshare is encouraged when possible.
Rideshare recommended
