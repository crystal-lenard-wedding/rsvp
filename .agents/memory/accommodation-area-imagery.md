---
name: Accommodation area imagery
description: Distinguish thematic area photos from verified hotel or location photos.
---

Accommodation section images are thematic area imagery. They should not be described as photos of the recommended hotels or documentary images of a specific location.

**Why:** The request was for photos representing lodging categories, not photos of individual properties. Presenting a thematic image as a real hotel or exact street would mislead guests. Full-width banners also need much wider source images than square generated assets: the image generator's "high" mode still returned 1024-pixel squares, which looked soft when expanded to the section width.

**How to apply:** The user explicitly does not want visible image notes or captions. Keep category images decorative, with empty alt text, and never claim they show a specific hotel or exact street. For full-width banners, use licensed sources at roughly twice the displayed width for sharp high-density screens. Verify the source, permission, and identity of a place before writing descriptive alt text.
