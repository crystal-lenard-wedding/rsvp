---
name: Accommodation area imagery
description: Distinguish thematic area photos from verified hotel or location photos.
---

Accommodation section images are generated, neighborhood-inspired mood images. They should not be described as photos of the recommended hotels or documentary images of a specific location.

**Why:** The request was for medium-sized photos representing lodging categories, not photos of individual properties. Presenting generated imagery as a real hotel or exact street would mislead guests.

**How to apply:** Keep an illustrative label when using these assets. If replacing them with actual hotel or neighborhood photography, verify the source, permission, and identity of the place before changing captions or alt text.
