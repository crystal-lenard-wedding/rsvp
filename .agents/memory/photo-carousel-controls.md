---
name: Photo carousel controls
description: Why the wedding site's desktop and mobile photo controls differ.
---

Keep the homepage photo controls readable regardless of image brightness. On desktop, a compact backed selector lets visitors jump directly to any photo. On mobile, use previous/next buttons and a count alongside swiping rather than adding another row of tiny dots as the collection grows.

**Why:** The original yellow active dot could disappear against bright photos, and a long row of small mobile targets became difficult to tap once the gallery reached 13 images.

**How to apply:** When adding more photos or revising carousel behavior, retain the dark backing and clear active state. Keep the count synchronized with slide changes, and preserve keyboard/button access and swipe navigation on mobile.
