description: Source and accuracy rules for recommending guest hotels near the wedding venue.
---

Use the couple's stated drive times for their established hotel picks. For newly added hotel options, verify that the property exists and use its official booking page, but do not guess a drive time; invite guests to check directions instead.

**Why:** The couple corrected several earlier estimates, and inaccurate travel guidance can mislead guests planning the wedding weekend.

**How to apply:** Whenever changing hotel recommendations or location labels, check the property's current name, neighborhood and official site. Do not replace the couple's stated estimates with unaudited online estimates.
