---
name: Hotel travel estimates
description: Source and accuracy rules for recommending guest hotels near the wedding venue.
---

Use the couple's stated drive times for all hotel picks, including newly added options when they provide an estimate. Verify the property exists and check its official information for descriptions. If the couple has not supplied a drive time, do not guess one; invite guests to check directions instead.

**Why:** The couple corrected several earlier estimates and later supplied times for new hotel picks. Inaccurate travel guidance can mislead guests planning the wedding weekend.

**How to apply:** Whenever changing hotel recommendations or location labels, check the property's current name, neighborhood and official site. Do not replace the couple's stated estimates with unaudited online estimates.
