---
name: Travel shortcut landing
description: Why travel shortcuts should show the beginning of a section instead of pinning its heading near the sticky navigation.
---

For Travel quick links, land at the section container with visible space below the sticky navigation, rather than aligning the heading close to the navigation.

**Why:** Guests reported that the shortcuts still scrolled too far after the headings had been made visible below the navigation and its measured mobile height had been accounted for. A technically visible heading was not enough to make the landing feel right.

**How to apply:** When changing Travel sections or their navigation, keep the section start and introductory context visible after a shortcut click on both desktop and phone. Avoid anchoring directly to a nested heading or card.
