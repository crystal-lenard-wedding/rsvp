Explore at your own pace

A few of our favorite ways to make a wedding weekend of it: courthouse details, harbor air, good things to eat, and a day out if you have one to spare.

What sounds like you?

A mini plan

A little of everything

Start with the Courthouse tower, wander toward the Public Market for a casual bite, then save the late afternoon for harbor-to-wharf views or a Butterfly Beach stroll. Check current schedules before you go.

Good places to begin

12 recommendations

🏛️

Courthouse clock tower

Downtown · Santa Barbara

A beautiful landmark with sweeping city-and-coast views from the clock tower. The courthouse offers free tours; check the official page for current tour details.

Tour details ↗
⛴️

Lil' Toot water taxi

Harbor to Stearns Wharf

A narrated, 25-minute harbor-to-wharf boat ride—an easy way to take in the waterfront. Check the schedule before heading to the harbor.

Ride & schedule info ↗
🌿

Santa Barbara Botanic Garden

A garden walk · Santa Barbara

A lovely change of pace among California native plants. Review visit information and reserve ahead as required by the garden.

Plan your visit ↗
🌺

Lotusland

Montecito · Garden visit

A singular garden estate with a remarkable collection of plants. Advance reservations are required; consult Lotusland for its 2026 season dates and visit details.

Reservations & season info ↗
🌊

Butterfly Beach

Montecito · By the water

Bring a layer and make time for a shoreline stroll. A simple, unhurried pick for golden-hour light and a little ocean air.

Santa Barbara beach guide ↗
🍷

The Funk Zone

Downtown · Food & wine

A colorful pocket near the waterfront for tasting rooms, art, and a choose-as-you-go meal. Make an afternoon of wandering between stops.

Explore the neighborhood ↗
🍋

Santa Barbara Public Market

Downtown · Easy group stop

A casual downtown food hall with a variety of vendors—handy when everyone wants something different. Check the market's site for current vendor and visit details.

Market details ↗
🍇

Santa Ynez wine country

Valleys, vineyards & small towns

Give the day to the vineyard scenery and tasting rooms, with a stop in a nearby town if that suits your pace. Plan a designated driver or transportation before tasting.

Explore wine country ↗
🪵

Cold Spring Tavern

Mountain stop · Food & history

A historic stagecoach stop tucked in the mountains. Seating is first come, first served; check the tavern's site for current days and details before setting out.

Tavern details ↗
🛶

Channel Islands National Park

A full-day island outing

Wild coast, sea caves, and wildlife make this a memorable bigger day out. Island access requires advance planning; check current park and boat-operator information before committing.

Plan an island visit ↗
🌁

Big Sur

LONGER OVERNIGHT / ROAD-TRIP EXTENSION

A spectacular stretch of California coast, but not an easy day trip from Santa Barbara. Treat it as a separate overnight plan and check Highway 1 conditions before choosing a route.

Check CA-1 road status ↗
🏡

Carmel-by-the-Sea

LONGER OVERNIGHT / ROAD-TRIP EXTENSION

A storybook coastal village of galleries, small lanes, and a beautiful beach—best saved for a dedicated overnight road trip, not squeezed into a Santa Barbara day.

Check CA-1 road status ↗

Planning a longer coastal drive? Big Sur and Carmel are overnight/road-trip extensions, not easy day trips. Highway 1 can be affected by closures; check Caltrans CA-1 status and route conditions before you go.
