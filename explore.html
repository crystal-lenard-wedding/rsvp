While You're Here

Explore the Area

Make a long weekend of it, from Santa Barbara's garden streets to Big Sur's dramatic cliffs.

🏛️
Santa Barbara Mission
SB · 5 min
The "Queen of the Missions", an 1786 Spanish colonial landmark surrounded by rose gardens.
🍷
Santa Ynez Wine Country
~40 min north
Rolling vineyard valleys and Solvang's charming Danish village.
🌊
Butterfly Beach
Montecito · 10 min
Quiet upscale beach, perfect for a golden-hour stroll.
🌁
Big Sur
~3 hr north on PCH
McWay Falls, Bixby Bridge, and one of the most dramatic coastlines on Earth.
🏡
Carmel-by-the-Sea
~4 hr north
Storybook village, white-sand beach, galleries, and California dining.
🌺
Lotusland Garden
SB · 15 min
Extraordinary private estate with 3,000+ exotic species. Book in advance.
🥘
The Funk Zone
SB · 8 min
Wine bars, craft breweries, galleries, and fresh tacos.
🦭
Channel Islands
~1.5 hr ferry
California's Galapagos: sea caves, marine wildlife, and pristine wilderness.
🏰
Hearst Castle
~1.5 hr north on PCH
Legendary hilltop palace with Neptune Pool, opulent rooms, and ocean panoramas.
🚲
Cabrillo Beachway
SB · 10 min
Rent bikes and cruise the palm-lined coastal path at sunrise.
